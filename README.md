## Overview

*Life Rays & Fragment Chains* is an ongoing theoretical research project exploring how experience, meaning, and cognition emerge through resonant encounters between systems.

Developed outside institutional settings, the project seeks to bridge phenomenology, systems theory, cognitive abstraction, and affective synchrony.

The **life ray** is introduced as a model of directional intentionality — a projection of life’s attention into shared and private space.  
**Fragment chains** describe how lived experiences organize into networks of resonance, producing continuity, memory, and identity.
