# Research Disclaimer

## Purpose

This platform is designed to collect behavioral data for research purposes related to memory structure, salience, and transduction modeling.

The study does not provide medical, psychological, or diagnostic services.

---

## Voluntary Participation

Participation is entirely voluntary.

Participants may discontinue at any time by closing the browser.

No compensation is provided.

---

## Data Collection

The study collects the following types of data:

- Short textual responses
- Self-reported ratings (valence, intensity, confidence)
- Response time (milliseconds)
- Delayed recall performance

No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected.

The system does not store:
- IP addresses
- Device fingerprints
- Location data
- Names or email addresses

Participants are instructed not to include personally identifiable information in free-text responses.

---

## Data Storage

Data is stored in encrypted cloud infrastructure within the selected AWS region.

Data is linked only via randomly generated participant identifiers.

No third-party analytics services are used.

---

## Risks

The study involves minimal risk.

Participants may encounter neutral memory recall tasks.
No sensitive or invasive procedures are used.

---

## Data Usage

Collected data may be used for:

- Parameter estimation
- Statistical modeling
- Model evaluation
- Academic publication or preprint dissemination

Only aggregated or anonymized results will be published.
