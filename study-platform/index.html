Cloud-Based Empirical Research Platform

A web-based system for collecting behavioral data to support empirical research on memory-related structures.

Purpose

This platform is designed to collect behavioral data for empirical research. The collected data supports the operationalization and analysis of parameters related to salience and transduction within a formal memory-structure framework.

The platform does not provide medical, psychological, or diagnostic services.

Study Overview

Participation consists of two phases:

  • Phase 1: Immediate response and rating task, including response time measurement.
  • Phase 2: Delayed recall task after a predefined time interval.

Collected Data

The following data types may be collected:

  • Short textual responses (non-identifying)
  • Emotional valence ratings
  • Intensity ratings
  • Confidence ratings
  • Response time measurements (milliseconds)
  • Delayed recall performance

Privacy and Data Protection

No personally identifiable information is collected.

  • No IP addresses are stored
  • No tracking cookies are used
  • No third-party analytics services are integrated

Data is stored in encrypted cloud infrastructure within the selected AWS region and is linked only via randomly generated participant identifiers.

Participation

Participation is voluntary. Participants may discontinue at any time by closing the browser window.

By proceeding to the study, participants confirm that they have read and understood the study information and consent to anonymous data processing for research purposes.
