Study Information

This platform collects behavioral data for research purposes. Participation is voluntary.

Study structure:

  • Phase 1: Immediate response and rating task (response time measured).
  • Phase 2: Delayed recall task after a predefined interval.

Collected Data

  • Short textual responses (participants should not include identifying information)
  • Valence, intensity, and confidence ratings
  • Response time measurements (milliseconds)
  • Delayed recall performance

Privacy

  • No personally identifiable information is collected
  • No IP addresses are stored
  • No tracking cookies or third-party analytics are used
  • Data is stored encrypted within the selected AWS region

Additional documentation is available in the public repository.
